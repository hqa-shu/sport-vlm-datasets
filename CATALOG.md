## Before preparing training data

1. Read the original paper and dataset card. Confirm tasks, annotation format, and official splits.
2. Check separate terms for annotations, videos, source broadcasts, and derived labels.
3. Preserve evaluation sets and record provenance for any transformations.
4. Report changed access conditions with a source URL and date. [Suggest a correction](https://github.com/hqa-shu/sport-vlm-datasets/issues/new?template=correction.yml).

This index does not download media or grant rights to third-party datasets. See [review details](README.md#scope-and-evidence) and [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md).
